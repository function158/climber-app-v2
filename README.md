# 🧗‍♂️ Climber App

**Climber App** is a simple tool for tracking your climbing workouts — especially **bouldering**.  
The app makes it easy to log your sessions, view detailed training statistics, and follow your progress over time.

---

## 💡 What You Can Do with Climber App

- **Log Your Sessions:**  
  Record the date, location, duration, exercises, and routes you climbed.

- **View Statistics:**  
  Get an overview of how much you’ve climbed — this week, this month, or *all time*.

- **Track Your Progress:**  
  The app automatically shows how many boulders you’ve completed and how much time you’ve spent climbing.

- **Sign In and Keep Your Data:**  
  Your training history is securely stored and linked to your personal profile.

- **Clean and Simple Design:**  
  The interface is built with **TailwindCSS** and **React**, providing a fast, modern experience on both mobile and desktop.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

After signing in with your email, you can:

1. Add a new climbing session  
2. Enter the time, location, routes, and any exercises  
3. Return to the dashboard to view your updated stats  

Everything is saved automatically, and your statistics update instantly when you add new sessions.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Climber.IO is built for boulderers and climbers who want to:
- Keep track of their training  
- See how they improve over time  
- Stay motivated by following their progress  

It’s **not** a social network or competition — just a simple and personal training tool.

And remember: climbing should always be fun first. 🧗‍♀️
